Escape Room [version 1.1]

Navigate through ajacent rooms clicking the arrows around the room, use the screen on the top right help visualize where you are on the map. A monster moves to another room when you move through one yourself. If you and the monster are in the same room, you lose. Win by finding the exit and escaping.

  • version 1.0 [inital release!]
  • version 1.1 [added sounds and music, as well as additional text!]
For the best experince, play with headphones, turn on full screen and in a dark or dimly lit room ;)
